# Economic / gameplay engine

**Status:** First engine pass. **Not tokenomics.** Token treated as **nonexistent**.  
**Kernel:** Entity · Relation · Intent · Occurrence · Domain.  
**Does not modify:** other corpus files.  
**Depends on:** `value.md` (W/P/E/S), `intents.md`, `production-while-away.md`, `control.md`, `principles.md`.

**Hard question:** What does the world **actually produce**, and can that support REAL VALUE IN → OUT — or only a closed game economy?

Be skeptical. **Do not assume mine → craft → market is enough.** Do not assume W goods have external buyers.

---

## 1. What the world produces

**W output** = **transformation of World records**, not a player IOU.

| Candidate | Δ in W | Why another actor cares | Become E? | Value without money? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raw lots / depleted vein | Lots `situated`; deposit down | Feed process, sell, deny | Yes | Yes (run a mill) |
| Refined / manufactured | Aggregate + lots | Higher use, building | Yes | Yes |
| Infrastructure / Sites | New/altered Site, `situated-at` | Capacity, chokepoint | Control/title **E** later | Yes (strategy) |
| Logistics / `connects` | Connectivity, impedance | Move lots, raid window | Access/rights **E** | Yes |
| Access | `access` relation | Enter without `control` | Yes | Yes |
| Information | **Not W** — Information writes | See `information.md` | Intel **E** | Yes (P) |
| Discoveries / remainder | New Place/Site/conditions | P12 | Knowledge **P**; not auto E | Yes |
| Blueprints / tech | **P** (or OPEN Technology) | Capability | Maybe E | Yes — **must not** be +% forever |
| Contracts/rights | **E** home undesigned | Claims | Are E | Only if useful |
| Services | No W object; **Intents** | Labor, haul, guard | Yes | Yes |
| Strategic position | Place/Site + relations | War/trade | Indirect | Yes |
| Events / opportunity | Conditions / new loci | Remainder | No Opportunity type | Yes |
| Reputation | **P** Identity OPEN | Trust | Fragile E | Yes |

**Not everything tradable.** Exploration wonder and remainder can stay **W/P-only**.

**Fatal if ignored:** **W lots are not automatically S.** “Iron exists” ≠ “outsider pays ETH.”

## 2. Transformations worth keeping

**Interesting (decisions, bottlenecks, sabotage):**

```
deposit → lot → refine/manufacture → consume in Site/infra
unknown → Assimilate → capability / blueprint (P) → better Intents
Place → access/`connects` → move lots under exposure → use/sale
control → ChangeAggregate → output in W → contest/interrupt
```

**Pointless complexity:** long craft trees that only exist to pad; refine steps with no **location, risk, or info** difference; tech that is only +% (P25).

Prefer **few transforms** with **where / who `control`s / who knows / what can break**.

---

## 3. What players do (no classes)

Using Intent families (`intents.md`): ChangePresence, Access, Control, Site, Aggregate, Connectivity; Assimilate / Intel holds; Oppose.

**Decisions:** where to be; what to learn; what to stand up; what to run; what to move; what to contest; what to tell.

**Emerge:** extractor, hauler, refiner, scout, fixer, raider, broker, guard — from **what they `control`, know, connect, and risk**, not a class pick.

Rust: risk, raid, loss of **W**. Civ: plan capacity, specialize, networks. Persistent **P** across wipes.

---

## 4. Scarcity that creates decisions

| Scarce | Interesting? |
| --- | --- |
| Deposits / lots | Yes, if logistics + consume |
| **Locations** / operable Sites | Yes (not infinite mills) |
| **Time / in-flight / attention** | Yes (opportunity cost) — not energy bars |
| Information (true, fresh) | Yes |
| `connects` / haul under exposure | Yes |
| **Remainder** (unowned future) | Yes — **must actually spawn (O6)** |
| Trust / counterparties | Yes for E |
| Control of **existing** Sites | Yes as **power** |
| Artificial unique cosmetics as RVI | Tempting; **not** a world economy |

**Frustration not interest:** fake unique IDs; gated verbs with no remainder; scarcity of **fun**.

---

## 5. Destruction / consumption (circulation)

**Needed:** veins deplete; lots **consumed** in craft/build/repair; infra **can** degrade or be damaged; routes fail; Sites transform/go; Intel **stales**; `control` lost; positions lost.

Without sinks, W **gluts** and E dies. **No rates here.**

Contracts expiring: when **E** exists.

---

## 6. Logistics

**Keep if** A→B is a **decision** (route, exposure, interrupt, timing) — then haul is **gameplay and E-service**.

**Drop if** it is only delay with no interactable `connects`. Then it is tedium.

Default: **logistics is in the engine**, not optional flavor — otherwise Sites are isolated +% boxes.

---

## 7. Information economy

Intel is valuable when it **changes Intents** (where the vein is, who `control`s, which `connects` is cut) **before** others know.

Worth more than the thing: **map of remainder**, **stale-trap**, **blueprint as capability**, exclusive window.

**Not** a gimmick if: stale, false, sealed/`holds`, discovery ≠ control, no omniscience dump.

Selling Intel is **E** among players — **not** RVI.

---

## 8. Control as economic power

`control` mine → operate → **W output** → maybe **E**. Contest/interrupt/Events/new remainder keep it from **title-of-the-planet**.

Compelling if output is **real W** and authority is **lossy**. Dead if `control` is a silent multiplier.

---

## 9. Persistent P

Knowledge of **how** and **kinds**; blueprints as **options**; reputation/access **OPEN**.

Across wipes: faster **re-entry and better choices**, not a permanent ore aura.

---

## 10. New-player entry

Veterans **dominate existing** Sites. New players need **remainder, stale maps, failed `control`, sinks, services they can sell (haul, scout), P14 so they are not verb-locked.**

If O6 does not spawn remainder, **P12 is a slogan** and the economy is a caste.

Veteran **P** stays meaningful (they still *know*); **W** rebuilds.

---

## 11. P2P exchange (reasons, not a protocol)

Lots, haul, repair, Intel, access, protection, **operate-for-another**, rights — **because specialization + logistics + info + risk**.

P2P **≠** RVI (`value.md`).

---

## 12. Backstop (P14)

**May:** low-quality / costly **baseline** so empty player market ≠ dead end (haul, basic process) — a **substitute**, often a **sink**.

**Must not:** print lots/`S`; be the **best** producer; replace player E; be a faucet for the idle.

If it creates W from **nothing**, it is **fake value**.

Economics of who pays: **OPEN** (do not invent treasury).

---

## 13. External demand — honest

**Why would an outsider pay S?**

| Candidate | They receive | Speculative? | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Play/access to the product** | Entertainment, agency in W | No | **Plausible RVI** (customers). Not a *world* export. |
| **In-world E** (capacity, lots, Intel, rights) | Use inside Dynasty | If only “someone will pay more” → **yes speculation** | Plausible **among players**; **outside** buyer **unspecified** |
| **Sponsorship** | In-world presence, events, audience | No, if they buy **attention/association** | **Plausible**; rights vs raids **O32** |
| **Fund production** | Share of **W/E** | If share is only resale to players → circular | Depends on **who buys the output** |
| **Investment-like exposure** | Claim on future S | **Usually speculation** | **Not** a substitute for demand |
| **Export of W goods to the real world** | Physical/digital utility **outside** | — | **No such good exists in the corpus** |

**Circular (reject as RVI):** they pay because the next player pays more.

**Current design has no demonstrated non-player, non-sponsor export.** Sponsorship and **willingness to pay to play/participate** are the only **named, non-circular families**. Neither is specified. **Do not pretend otherwise.**

## 14. REAL VALUE OUT

Justified only after **real W/E transition** + **authorized S** (`value.md`).

Could be: withdraw unused S; pay a player for a service **from S the payer brought**; sponsor spend flowing to operators — **if** that S **entered** for §13 reasons.

Without §13, RVO is **returning deposits** (not profit from production).

**Not designed.**

## 15. Protocol share

World produces **W**. Players produce **Intents + P**. P2P is **E**. Protocol **captures** only if it provides **clearing, backstop, match, world instance, settlement** — a **fee for a function**, not “because we exist.”

Fee on P2P **≠** RVI. Percentages **not designed**.

## 16. Smallest loop

**Gameplay/E loop (token = 0, no outsider):**

```
Remainder / vacant Site
  → effort, risk, info, Control
  → ChangeAggregate / logistics
  → W lots & infra
  → consume / degrade / lose
  → specialize → P2P E
```

Risk, scarcity, info, competition, remainder, destruction: **in this loop**.

**RVI/RVO loop:** **not closed.** It **requires** §13. Until then the thesis is **constitutional, not operational**.

---

## 17. Token = 0 / token absent — **mandatory**

**Yes, the game can still be** strategically interesting, internally coherent, worth playing, worth **P2P E** in W goods/Intel/services, capable of **W output**.

**Capable of attracting external demand?** **Only** via §13 families (play/sponsor/etc.), **independent of a token**. Token absence **does not** create those buyers; **token presence would not either**.

If Dynasty were **only** interesting *because* of a token, this pass **fails**. It does not.

---

## 18. One whale

**Can:** buy scale, labor, Intel, many `control` contests, glut local E, stress markets.

**Must not (structure, not a tax table):** own **unhappened** remainder; auto-win mastery (explore, decide, false Intel); loot **S** deposits (P15); freeze the map forever (wipe + O6 + contestable `control`).

**Gaps:** Intel-for-S (P11); hired issuers; if remainder fails, whale **does** own “everything that exists.”

---

## 19. Wipe

**Disappears:** W (Sites, lots, `control`, `connects`).  
**Persists:** P; **S** (must not toy-wipe).  
**E:** **O20**.  
**Regenerates:** new remainder, rebuild `control`.  
**Veteran edge:** P (and S to redeploy as **power**).  
**Restart:** yes, as a **new W economy**; not as kept factories.

---

## 20. W → E → S boundary

**W→E:** listing/title/contract on a **real** W/P thing.  
**E→S:** **authorized settlement only.** World Intents **do not write S**. Client **does not invent** lots or S. No double settlement.

**Architecture not designed.**

---

## 21. Missing / fatal

**The gameplay engine can exist** (W transform + logistics + info + contestable `control` + consume + remainder).

**The crypto-economic thesis cannot yet be demonstrated.** Missing is **not a token**. Missing is:

> **A specified outsider (or player-as-customer) who pays S because they receive something other than “the next speculator.”**

Smallest missing **process** (not a kernel primitive): **ExternalDemand** — who pays, **what they get** (access-to-play, sponsorship object, or an export **that does not exist yet**). Until that is designed, RVI is a **principle**.

Do not invent “game iron is a real commodity.”
